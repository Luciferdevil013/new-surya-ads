Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

This policy explains how Your Business ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this page or contact us through WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you tap the WhatsApp button and message us, we receive your WhatsApp phone number, your profile name, and the contents of your messages.
  • Usage data. Standard technical information such as your browser type, device type, approximate region, referring page, and the campaign parameters in the link you arrived from.
  • Advertising cookies. If advertising measurement is enabled, the Meta Pixel may set cookies to measure the performance of our ads. See "Advertising and analytics" below.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the products or services you asked about.
  • To measure which advertisements bring genuine enquiries.
  • To keep records required for accounting, tax, or legal compliance.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and analytics

We advertise on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). Where enabled, the Meta Pixel records that a visit or an enquiry occurred so we can measure ad performance and reach similar audiences. Meta processes this data under its own Privacy Policy. You can manage your ad preferences in your Meta account settings.

WhatsApp messages

Conversations take place inside WhatsApp and are subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy. We only message you in response to a conversation you started, or where you have agreed to receive messages from us. You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP or by blocking our number.

Retention

We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal obligations, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Write to us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.
